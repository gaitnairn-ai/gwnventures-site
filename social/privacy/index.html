Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026 · Last Updated: April 27, 2026

SOCIAL is operated by GWN Ventures LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding your data.

SOCIAL is a social networking app designed around personal connections. Our current data practices are described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — required for account creation via email sign-up, or provided by Apple or Google during sign-in (Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real email)
  • Display name and username — provided by you during onboarding
  • Date of birth — used solely to verify you meet the minimum age requirement (13+); stored on your profile but not displayed to other users
  • Profile photo — optional; stored in our file storage system
  • Bio — optional

We do not receive or store your Apple or Google password when you sign in with those providers.

User-Generated Content

  • Photos — uploaded as part of posts; compressed on your device before upload
  • Videos — uploaded as part of posts; compressed on your device before upload
  • Audio — captured as part of video recording only; SOCIAL does not record audio independently
  • Text content — post captions, comments, direct messages, group messages, and introduction messages
  • Group names and group images

Identifiers

  • User ID — a unique identifier generated by our authentication system when you create your account
  • Device install ID — a randomly generated identifier used to deliver over-the-air app updates to your device; this is not a persistent device identifier and does not track you across apps
  • Push notification token — a device-specific token used to deliver push notifications to your device; generated by Apple's Push Notification service and does not contain personal information

Location Data

  • Precise location (GPS coordinates) — collected only when you actively use the "Find Nearby" feature to discover friends in your physical vicinity. Your coordinates are transmitted to our server, stored briefly, and then automatically deleted. Your exact coordinates are not displayed to other users. Location is collected only while the app is in the foreground and only when you have granted the "When In Use" location permission.

Diagnostics

  • Crash data — when the app crashes or encounters a serious error, we collect stack traces, device model, operating system version, and app version via our crash reporting service (Sentry). We scrub personally identifiable information — including email addresses, IP addresses, passwords, and authentication tokens — from crash reports before transmission.
  • Error logs — non-crashing errors (failed network requests, rendering errors) are logged with your user ID, error type, source, and app/OS version. These logs never include message content, post content, or other user-generated text.

Future Features

If we introduce in-app purchases in the future, we may collect purchase history and transaction identifiers as necessary to provide and support those purchases. We will update this Privacy Policy before collecting any new categories of data.

When you delete your account (Settings > Delete Account), we delete your account data from our active systems, including your profile, posts, messages, friend connections, group memberships, and introduction requests. This action cannot be undone. Some data may persist in automated backup systems for a limited period and in third-party services (such as crash reports already submitted to Sentry) until their normal retention periods expire. Error logs associated with your account are anonymized by removing your user identifier.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure (hosted on Amazon Web Services). Authentication tokens are stored on your device using the iOS Keychain (via secure storage) and are not transmitted except during authentication. All data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by our infrastructure providers.

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including database-level access controls that enforce visibility rules, server-side validation of all data-modifying operations, time-limited access to stored media, and rate limiting to prevent abuse.

No system is perfectly secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

All users

  • Access your data within the app (your profile, posts, and messages are visible to you)
  • Correct your data (edit your profile, edit or delete your posts)
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Settings > Delete Account)
  • Revoke permissions for camera, photos, location, and notifications via iOS Settings at any time

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Delete your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell or share your personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act
  • Non-discrimination — we will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights

To submit a request, contact us at the email address listed below.

European Economic Area residents (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Our legal bases for processing under GDPR:

  • Contract performance — account data, content, messages (necessary to provide the service)
  • Legitimate interest — crash data, error logs, device install ID (necessary to maintain and improve the app)
  • Consent — precise location (you opt in each time you use Find Nearby via the iOS permission prompt)

To exercise your rights, contact us at the email address listed below.

7. Children's Privacy

SOCIAL is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We collect date of birth during account creation solely to verify that users meet the minimum age requirement of 13 years.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete the account and all associated data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has created an account on SOCIAL, please contact us at the email address listed below.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using SOCIAL, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations. Our service providers maintain appropriate safeguards for international data transfers, including compliance with applicable data protection frameworks.
